## Review method and notes

For the selected-data/authority items, trace the independently required judgment into the specification scenario, contract obligation, responsible actor, supplied datum, rejection boundary and subsequent use. Identify the actual text supporting each link. A matching FR tag, acyclic task graph, reference-model result or old review cannot replace this check.

Use the already specified distinctions to test the written design: would it permit replacing a chosen nonidentity component by identity, treating a valid nonunique choice as ambiguity, executing after a mandatory datum is omitted, waiting for arbitrary theorem nonexistence before reporting that omission, or letting a leaf define the criterion its implementation satisfies? A document that permits one of those substitutions fails the corresponding item even when its coverage metrics are complete. These are requirements-reading checks, not new independent mathematical specimens or assertions.

The reviewer owns checklist state. Record precise findings and source locations beside the affected item; leave it unchecked until that review responsibility is discharged. Editing this checklist does not mark it passed. Requirements-quality review, source formalization, native preservation, independent computational assessment and complete delivery are different claims.

`$speckit-implement` reads this checklist; an unchecked item is not an approval. The built-in `checklists/requirements.md` has its separate `$speckit-specify`/`$speckit-clarify` lifecycle; this checklist does not inherit its results. A documentation/remediation assignment does not authorize implementation, CI changes or advancement of implementation markers.

The stable checklist remains CHK001–CHK040; this correction sharpens its existing criteria rather than creating a second checklist or approval process. Historical reports retain their actual version/scope; none automatically approves later consolidated bodies.
