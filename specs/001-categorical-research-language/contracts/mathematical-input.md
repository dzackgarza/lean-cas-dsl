Leaves register and compute against the released obligations; they do not supply mathematical definitions, proofs, certificates, checkers, new denotations or acceptance. It is retained with the selected reading, not derived from installed-provider success, priority, cost, route length or registration order. Referencing an independently formalized comparison is likewise not authoring that comparison. The declaration does not acquire authority to change categories, laws, domains or admissible structures.

Retain `M; Γ ⊢ t : T` with the full ordered local/universe context, hypotheses, selected instances/structures, actual diagrams, routes, cells and qualifications. Validate accepted source declarations with the existing safety/axiom rules: no loose variables, unresolved expression/universe metavariables, unsafe declarations or unauthorized axioms. Reductions require the independently established adequacy for their promised use. Existing ordinary RX08/RX-LIFT work is not gated by unrelated higher foundations.

Finite storage/display does not replace an infinite object. ## Fixed results and independently declared choices

A fixed-result request keeps the result prescribed by its accepted mathematical declaration.
A function defined using `Classical.choice` is still that fixed function; its implementation
does not thereby gain permission to return an arbitrary candidate of the same Lean type.

A permitted-choice request requires the independently accepted candidate family, admissibility
condition and dependent continuation, with the required suppliers and availability stages.
Preparation retains those declared boundaries and the actual chosen data through later uses.
Admission of the returned candidate is structural: its mandatory fields, roles, layout and scope
(section 4.4). The admissibility condition is a mathematical statement. In the kernel, only a
procedure registered in lean-categories settles it (constitution C22); with no such procedure it
stays a claim for independent assessment.
A result type, a runtime supplier tag or a successful computation cannot supply missing choice
authority. A missing accepted input is a task of lean-categories. The task list names it before
its consumer, and the workstream does it there as a formalization author, under
[the authorship rule](../../../.specify/memory/constitution.md#independent-authorship-and-permitted-inputs-c1-c9-c10):
from the mathematics, not shaped to the kernel. The kernel, the contract and a leaf do not supply it.
