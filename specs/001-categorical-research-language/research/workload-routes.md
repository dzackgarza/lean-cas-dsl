## Method

Rows. A declaration name in a reading is mapped to the catalogue row that names it (1043 declaration names, 649 rows). 14 names that the catalogue does not name are explained in `evidence.py` (`SUPPLEMENT`) from the lean-categories source and Mathlib. A statement without a recorded reading takes the rows of a kernel reading, and otherwise the rows that `textmatch.py` matches in its source text and in the bindings that it uses.

Categories on the route (`route.py`):

- **R1 owner.** Each row brings its owner category, and the categories in which its declared parameters are read (the parameter types of its Lean signature: `mor.sets.matrix_det` takes `K : CommRingCat`, so it brings `cat.commutative_rings`).
- **R2 carrier.** When the statement is an equality or a decided predicate, or names a row of `cat.sets` that acts on elements, each object read in a category C brings a path from C to `cat.sets`: the functor rows that the statement names itself, otherwise the registered `refines` route of the objects of C, otherwise the shortest structural path. A settled typing judgement brings no carrier.
- **R3 inherited operation.** A row whose owner O is not the category of an object of the statement brings the structural path from such a category to O.
- **R4 built from.** A constructed category brings the categories that its constructor takes, and a family category brings the categories of its parameters.

Optional categories are not counted. They record a registered alternative: the other route of a cell, the base of a refined operation, the prerequisites of a computation derivation.

The check of the rules on the example of the specification: rows `obj.rings.integers`, `op.rings.mul`, `num.rings`, `mor.sets.equality` give sets, magmas, semigroups, monoids, rings, with semirings optional (`python3 -I route.py`).

Layers. The longest chain to `cat.sets` along structural functors and classifier forgets reproduces the layer table of the plan (layers 0 to 8).

## Fields of a record

`workload-routes.json` is a list. Each record has `id`, `file`, `line`, `statement`, `source` (`permanent`, `extension`, `extension-negative`, `extension-scope`), `scope`, `rows`, `categories`, `confidence`, `unresolved`. It also has: `optional_categories`; `evidence` (the file that gave the rows); `question_kind`; `why` (the rule and the row behind each category); `notes` (assumptions of the text match); `report` (outcome, detail, address rows and readings of the kernel worktree); `report_adds`, `notation_adds`, `current_adds` (categories that current evidence adds); `holds_with_empty_manifest`; `categories_kernel_module_carrier`; `text_match` (the rows and categories of the text match, for every statement).
